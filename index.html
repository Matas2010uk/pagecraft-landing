FAQ

Questions, answered.

The stuff people usually ask before getting started.

How long does it take? +
Most pages are designed, built and live within 7 days of our first call. Bigger projects may take a little longer — we will tell you upfront.
Do I need hosting? +
We can host it for you from £5 a month, or hand everything over so you can host it yourself. Your choice — no lock-in either way.
Can I edit it myself? +
Yes. We build it so you can update text, photos and prices yourself without touching code. We show you how before we finish.
Do you do SEO? +
Every page includes SEO basics — titles, descriptions, fast loading and mobile-friendly design. That is the foundation. For ongoing SEO campaigns, we can point you to trusted partners.
What if I don't like it? +
You see a free mockup before you pay anything. If it is not right, we tweak it until it is. No risk, no pressure.
